Tips

  • ⭐ Mermaid show priority seats
    Must-do: buy priority seats for the 14:00 Sea Shell mermaid show (paid add-on) in the VinWonders app or at the Sea Shell counter as soon as you enter in the morning – they fill fast.
  • Fastpass for riders
    ~US$11.60 for 4 rides – worth it on a Saturday with only 1 h per shift. Do the biggest rides first.
  • Viking rapids
    Crocs, poncho or spare T-shirt for the kids. iPhone in the waterproof pouch.
  • Before your ride shift
    Hand phones and bags to the other shift.
  • Morning check
    Confirm show times in the VinWonders app (hotline 1900 6677, press 2) – schedules can change.
  • Bring
    Caps, sunscreen, water, snacks, power bank. Wear the Gel-Excite 10.
